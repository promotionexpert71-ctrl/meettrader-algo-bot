How It Works

Three moving parts, one discipline.

No guesswork, no emotion — the bot follows a fixed set of entry, exit and sizing rules on every trade it takes.

Connect

Link your trading account

Your broker account stays in your name and under your control. The bot is granted trade execution only — never withdrawal access.

Execute

The algo trades your rules

Positions are opened and closed automatically across major, minor and cross pairs, sized according to preset risk limits — day and night.

Review

Track every trade, in real time

Every fill is logged to your statement — entry, exit, lot size, and P/L — so you can review exactly what the bot did and why.
